# Underdeep — Exploration, dungeons, ruins and artifacts (Stage 9)

Public, spoiler-free reference. Identities of secret places, puzzle solutions, trap placements, the true history
behind the records, artifact effects and hidden advancements are in private design material
(`docs/private/EXPLORATION.md`) and are deliberately not described here.

## 1. Goals
Exploration is the reason to leave home. The caverns hold the remains of older peoples: small ruins along every
road, landmarks visible from far away, dungeons with mechanisms that still work, and hidden rooms that reward
attention. Everything is placed deterministically from the seed, is never re-rolled, and is fair to a group of
4–6 players exploring together.

## 2. Content (as implemented, v0.10.0)
| Kind | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ruins and abandoned places | 30 families | minor (most common) and major (towns, halls, libraries) |
| Landmarks | 16 families | large, rare, visible; some hold hidden rooms |
| Dungeons | 20 families | 3 scales: regional, major, exceptional; puzzles, traps, guards, a heart room |
| Secret designs | 10 standalone + 17 hidden rooms inside other designs | discovered only by entering them |
| Puzzle mechanics | 16 types (+ key locks) | 37 puzzle instances in the authored designs |
| Traps | 11 mechanics | always telegraphed; disarmable; severity configurable |
| Travel encounters | 18 | regional, with cooldowns; never in settlements or near player bases |
| Artifacts and treasures | 38 + 3 valuables | accessories (3 unique per world), weapons, utility items, relic blocks |
| Historical records | 54 | inscriptions and loose fragments, copied into the journal |
| Rumours | 28 | told by inhabitants, point to real undiscovered places |
| Advancements | 38 (new "Expedition" tab) | some hidden |
| Handbook | +115 pages | exploration guides, per-place pages unlocked by discovery, journal |

Layouts are authored in Java (`explore/design/Ruins`, `Landmarks`, `Dungeons`, `Secrets`) with a small vocabulary
(`Kit`: rooms, corridors, spiral wells, arches, pillars, furniture, weathering, plus puzzle and trap helpers) and
culture palettes (`Style`). Each layout varies with the site seed (rotation, weathering, decoration, optional
rooms) while keeping its puzzles solvable. Family metadata (names, regions, layers, sizes, weights, audio
context) is data in `data/underdeep/exploration/content.json`, generated by `tools/assets/stage9.py`.

## 3. Placement (pure function of seed + terrain model)
- A jittered grid per layer (80 blocks upper, 96 deep). Each cell holds a site with probability 0.40 (upper) /
  0.30 (deep) × `density`. The family is drawn by weight, with a ×2.2 bonus for families native to the region.
- Fit check on the terrain model (no chunk loads): floor relief, headroom, no lava floor, water only for aquatic
  designs, solid rock for parts built below the floor, not inside a settlement plan or the starting area, no
  dungeon within 220 blocks of world spawn.
- Overlaps with neighbouring cells (±2) are resolved by priority, so the result never depends on query order.
- `ExplorationMap` (per world) answers `sitesIn`, `siteAt`, `sitesNear`, `byId` and caches layouts (LRU 40).
- `SitePlacer` writes only the part of a layout inside the current chunk (3×3 window rule), sets loot tables on
  shared containers, adds footings and seals fluids at openings.
- Sites generate only in worlds created with generator v4+ (config `inOlderWorlds` allows new chunks of older
  worlds; sites at the border of already generated land may be cut).
- Measured (seed 42): ~780 sites within 2000 blocks of spawn over both layers; every one of the 15 ecosystems
  has sites; 60 of 76 families appear in that area (the rarest need a wider search).

## 4. Persistent state
- `ExplorationState` (one `PersistentState` per server): per site — puzzle states, reward claims, jammed traps,
  woken guards, discoverers, completion; per world — awarded unique artifacts, warded areas, reported findings.
- `ExplorerData` (player attachment `underdeep:explorer`): discovered sites and hidden rooms, completed
  expeditions, records read, artifacts found, leads (from rumours and charts), cultures met, last encounter.
- Mechanisms need no block entities: `SiteLayout` indexes puzzle elements, seals, traps, caches and inscriptions
  by world position, so the layout itself is the source of truth and only player-caused changes are stored.

## 5. Puzzle framework
`Mechanisms` dispatches use/step events on mechanism blocks to the puzzle that owns that position. A puzzle has a
type, element positions, a solution (data, derived from the site seed) and the seals it opens. Solving is
validated on the server; seals open for everybody; unsolved puzzle elements are protected from breaking.
Puzzle types: dial matching, plate sequences, braziers (all / in order / extinguish), offerings, chime order,
lever patterns, basin filling, light beams with mirrors and lenses, twin plates (two players or a weight), memory
pillars, mosaics, timed gates, power restoration, counterweights and key locks. Each puzzle shows a hint when
inspected (inscriptions nearby help). Puzzles are designed to be solvable by one player and faster with several.

## 6. Traps and danger
Traps are always telegraphed (sound, particles, visible vents or cracks) and can be disarmed or blocked. Vents
only cycle while a player is near. Hazard traps feed the Stage 7 exposure meters (armour protection applies).
Guards (vanilla undead) wake once when their room is entered. `trapSeverity` scales damage (0 = warnings only);
`guardians` disables guards.

## 7. Rewards and multiplayer fairness
| Policy | Container | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| SHARED | ordinary chests/barrels | vanilla loot tables, rolled once (whoever opens first) |
| PERSONAL | expedition coffer | each player takes their own share once (claim per player) |
| UNIQUE | relic pedestal | one claim per site; a unique artifact is awarded once per world |
| KEY | key pedestal | each player may take one key, so a lost key never blocks progress |
Claims are recorded before items are given, in the same server tick, so simultaneous use cannot duplicate
rewards. Claims survive restarts and are independent of puzzle state. Completing a dungeon (reaching its heart
with the required puzzles solved) credits everyone present once.

## 8. Discovery, journal and Handbook
- `Discovery` runs once per second per player: entering a site discovers it (chat line, advancement event,
  Handbook page unlock), entering a hidden room discovers it, guarded rooms wake guards, the heart completes the
  expedition. `minorDiscoveryMessages` silences messages for minor ruins.
- Records (inscription stones, lore fragments) are copied into the journal. The journal is a Handbook category
  sent from the server (`underdeep:explore_journal`): places with live distance/bearing, leads, records,
  artifacts. Per-place Handbook pages unlock only on discovery (`req: site:<id>` / `secret:<id>`).
- The delver's compass (Stage 8) also lists the eight nearest discovered places.

## 9. Inhabitants: rumours, charts, identification, reports
Through dialogue (`ExploreDialogue`): rumours (once per day per inhabitant; they name a real undiscovered place
and add a lead), surveyor's charts (cartographers/navigators, 6 emeralds: marks a nearby site), identification
of encrusted relics (3 emeralds), and reporting findings (the settlement treasury pays once per site/record per
settlement, through audited Stage 6 transfers). Companions comment on places they enter with the player.

## 10. Travel encounters
`TravelEncounters` picks from regional encounters on a timer scaled by `encounterFrequency`, with per-encounter
and per-player cooldowns, at a safe spot away from settlements, containers, beds, doors and warded areas. The
warding bell (a craftable block) wards an area around a base against encounters.

## 11. Configuration (`config/underdeep.json`, section `exploration`)
`enabled`, `inOlderWorlds`, `density`, `dungeonDensity` (changing either changes future placement: use for fresh
worlds), `encounterFrequency`, `trapSeverity`, `minorDiscoveryMessages`, `guardians`.

## 12. Operator commands
`/underdeep explore here | near [radius] | journal | me | encounter <id>` (permission-gated; `near` and `here`
reveal positions and identities — for administrators and testing only).

## 13. Performance
Placement queries are pure and cached; resolving all sites within 2000 blocks takes ~1.3 s once (diagnostics
only — gameplay queries a few cells). Discovery is a 1 Hz bounded check over sites near each player. Vents tick
only near players. No chunks are force-loaded; no block entities are added for mechanisms.

## 14. Contracts for later stages
- **Stage 10 (combat, bosses):** some dungeons contain reserved rooms behind seals with no puzzle; they are empty
  space today and are the intended locations for later encounters (`Canvas.reserved`, `SiteLayout.reservedBox`).
  Guards use vanilla undead; Stage 10 may replace them through `Traps.wake`. Dungeon completion is reported
  with `Advancements.event(player, "expedition_completed", family)`.
- **Stage 11 (audio, polish):** each family declares an audio context (`ruin`, `ruin_major`, `landmark`,
  `dungeon`, `dungeon_major`, `dungeon_exceptional`, `secret`); the server sends the player's current context
  (`underdeep:explore_context`, only for discovered places, `none` outside) for the soundtrack system. Visual
  backlog: procedural placeholder textures for mechanism blocks and artifacts; relic blocks use simple models.

## 15. Testing
`SUITE=stage9 scripts/selftest.sh <seed> <dir> create` (~2–3 min): static audit of every design over 3 seeds
(walkability from the entrance to every room, puzzle data, light paths, extents), placement statistics and
determinism, generated chunks compared with layouts, and in-world mechanics with all 20 dungeons placed in a test
area (every puzzle solved through real interactions, rewards, traps, guards, discovery, completion, persistence,
records, inhabitants, encounters, artifacts, warding, spoiler visibility). Results are in `docs/TESTING.md`.
