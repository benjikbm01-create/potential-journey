# PRIVATE — Stage 5 inhabitants (spoilers)

Design notes the player should discover in play rather than read:
- Each culture's greeting style and sayings (`Topics` "culture" topic) are written as small hints of the
  histories in `CIVILIZATIONS.md`; later stages can add sayings that only elders know (age ELDER) and that refer
  to the hidden places in `HIDDEN_DISCOVERIES.md`.
- The DISCOVERY knowledge kind is reserved for news of the player's major finds (Stage 6+: when a player reveals
  a hidden place, witnesses learn it and it spreads); it is deliberately never pre-seeded, so no inhabitant can
  leak a secret before the player has found it.
- Companion memories of biomes (NEW_PLACE) are the hook for companions recognising places tied to their
  culture's legends in later stages.
