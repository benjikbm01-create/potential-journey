# Private notes — Stage 8 (spoiler-sensitive; never surface in public docs or reports)

- Discovery-sensitive items (`data/underdeep/guide/visibility.json` → `sensitive`): `ancient_seed`, `relic_sprout`,
  `relic_fruit`, `umbral_bloom`, `umbral_petal`. They are obtainable (the audit proves it) but their Handbook pages
  are sent to a player only after that player has held the item. Their acquisition routes are the Stage 2 hidden
  discoveries described in `HIDDEN_DISCOVERIES.md`.
- Hidden advancements (tab "adventure"): `relic` ("Something Very Old", obtain the relic fruit, challenge, 100 xp)
  and `dark_bloom` ("Bloom in the Dark", obtain an umbral petal, goal, 60 xp). Descriptions are "???" until earned.
- Deferred items: none at the end of Stage 8. Future secret gear (Stage 9–10 encounters, deep Abyss gear) must be
  added to `deferred` until its stage implements the route, and listed here.
- The hazard-protection numbers assume players combine region gear (rimesteel/fur for the Everfrost, galvanic for
  Stormstone, nacre for the flooded deeps, shellplate for spores); the umbralite tier is the intended key to the
  deepest Abyss areas planned for Stage 9–10. Umbralite ore needs a diamond-tier (embersteel/rimesteel/starsilver)
  pickaxe, which in turn needs a trip into a hazard region: that loop is deliberate.
- The netherite smithing template alternative (from umbralite) exists only so that the vanilla netherite route is
  not dead underground; it is intentionally expensive.
