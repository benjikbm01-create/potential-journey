# PRIVATE — Stage 6 economy (spoilers)

- The `NEW_DEPOSIT` event and `minerals` pool growth are the intended hook for Stage 9 discoveries: a player who
  reveals a hidden deposit could permanently raise a region's capacity.
- Exceptional species (see `HIDDEN_DISCOVERIES.md`) are deliberately excluded from all goods, gathering and
  trade, so no merchant can sell or mention them before players find them.
