# Private notes — Stage 7 (spoiler-sensitive; never surface in public docs or reports)

- The reserved materials listed publicly (cryolite, galvanite, storm lichen, glow gland, bloodcap, deathbell) are
  intended for Stage 8 gear that lets players push deeper into the Abyss and Stormstone without the Stage 7
  hazards; the hazard tuning (gloom, pressure, fumes) assumes that gear will exist.
- Starlily nectar and starlit porridge are the Astral recipes; they are deliberately only teachable by Astral cooks
  (or found by experimenting) to give a reason to reach the Starlight settlements.
- The purging draught is intentionally the strongest cure with the longest cooldown and a side effect; keep it
  rare in settlement stock.
