# PRIVATE — Underdeep deep lore (SPOILERS)

## The Hollowing
The caverns are not natural. Ages ago a surface civilisation, the Vaultwrights, sealed themselves
beneath the world when the sky "went out". They carved the first great chambers with resonant crystal
engines — the luminite of the Crystal Deep is the residue of those engines, still faintly humming.
The bedrock roof is their work: a sky-seal. Nobody alive knows what lies above it, and the world should
never confirm it.

## The Rootwild
The Rootwild forests grow from the roots of a single impossibly old surface tree whose trunk was
severed when the seal closed. The great hanging roots in Rootwild ceilings are its living remains.
Late-game: following the thickest roots upward leads to the Heartwood, a sealed chamber where the root
still pulses.

## The Abyss
The lava sea at the bottom of the Abyss is warm because something sleeps in it. Sculk spreads from its
dreams. The deep-layer civilisation that once lived at its rim chose silence (sculk sensors) as
religion. Their ruins should be discovered in Stage 9, their reason in Stage 10.

## Cultural seeds (for Stage 4)
- A fungal culture that believes spores carry the memories of the dead.
- Lake folk who build floating towns and refuse to touch stone.
- Ash-smiths who keep the old Vaultwright forges burning without understanding them.
